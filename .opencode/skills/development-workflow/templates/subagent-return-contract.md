# Subagent Return Contract

Require this structure from delegated work.

```text
Result:

Files inspected:

Files changed:

Evidence:

Verification run:

What passed:

What failed or was not run:

Assumptions:

Risks:

Out-of-scope findings:

Recommended next action:
```

Rules:

- Do not claim completion without evidence.
- Do not hide skipped verification.
- Do not include unrelated exploration unless it affects the task.
